# Slot B thread: NN/g survey on AI in UX

**Status:** READY · **Post at:** 18:45 IST (suggested) · **Source:** [NN/g, "AI in UX: Work Is Faster, But Practitioners Have Mixed Feelings" (2026-10-23)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-in-ux-survey-26/)

## Tweet 1 (223 chars) · image: `images/hook.png`

NN/g surveyed 521 UX practitioners about AI at work. They rated the time it saves them at 4.2 out of 5.

How they feel about using it averaged 3.57, barely above neutral. Faster work and lukewarm people, in the same survey.

## Tweet 2 (258 chars)

The comments explain the gap. The most common complaint was unreliable output and the time spent checking it.

Others described AI mandates, tracked usage, and fixing unusable prototypes a product manager made, while being expected to work as fast as the AI.

## Tweet 3 (261 chars)

NN/g's conclusion is the line I'd pin on every team wall: "a prototype generated in minutes that costs a designer days to make usable is a net loss."

The time saved rarely stays with the person who saved it. It turns into more output or someone else's cleanup.

## Tweet 4 (260 chars, URL counted as 23)

So stop counting how many people use AI. Count whether work reaches users faster and better.

In the survey, the people whose work got better put the saved time back into the work.

Full survey by Maria Rosala and Rachel Banawa at NN/g: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-in-ux-survey-26/
